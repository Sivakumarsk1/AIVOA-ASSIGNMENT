I am writing to report a serious quality concern regarding your product Amoxicillin 500mg Tablets (Brand: AmoxiCure Plus).

Product Details:
- Product Name: Amoxicillin Trihydrate Tablets IP
- Strength: 500mg
- Batch/Lot Number: AMX-2026-0847
- Manufacturing Date: March 12, 2026
- Expiry Date: February 2028
- Quantity Affected: 150 tablets (5 bottles of 30 tablets each)

Complaint Description:
During routine dispensing at our pharmacy (Apollo Pharmacy, Chennai Branch - Adyar), a pharmacist noticed dark metallic specks embedded within several tablets from the above batch. Upon further inspection of additional bottles from the same batch, approximately 8 out of 30 tablets in each bottle showed similar contamination.

The metallic particles appear to be small, dark gray/black specks approximately 0.5-1mm in size, visible on the tablet surface and when broken in half. We have immediately quarantined all remaining stock of this batch (approximately 23 bottles).

No patient has consumed the affected tablets as this was caught during inspection before dispensing.

We received this batch from MedSupply Distributors on June 28, 2026 (Invoice #MS-INV-78234). The storage conditions at our pharmacy have been maintained at controlled room temperature (20-25°C) with 45-55% RH throughout.

We are retaining 3 affected bottles for your investigation and can ship the samples upon request. Photographs of the affected tablets have been taken and can be shared.

Please treat this as a high-priority complaint and advise on immediate next steps.
